FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get licensed?

Go to Applications, submit the License Application, and wait for staff review.

Can I apply for staff?

Yes. Use the Staff Application and answer every question honestly and with detail.

What if I was infracted unfairly?

Use the Appeal Application and include your perspective, evidence, and context.

Where do I ask questions?

Open a support ticket in the Discord server.
